What changed
  The moonlit title painting Splaze approved (Krampus in the tree-corner,
  candy-cane logo A) is now the official cover and the in-game title screen.
  Krampus's cut-out edge was lightly blended into the night sky, and the
  stray hair wisps in the patched sky were cleaned. Composition, crew and
  logo placement are unchanged.
  Official files:
    art/cover/north-pole-official-cover-bg.png      (painting only)
    art/cover/north-pole-official-cover.png         (logo + YEAR ONE, no buttons)
    art/cover/north-pole-official-cover-no-year.png (logo only)
  The title screen draws the painting and a 1280×720 candy-cane logo overlay
  at native scale (no 320-buffer upscale, no smoothing when the canvas is
  1280×720). The old yellow serif "NORTH POLE" logo is gone. PLAY, CONTINUE
  and OPTIONS still work, as does the Esc menu (Music, SFX, Lighting).
  SFX are unchanged (not wired in this update).

How to play (web)
  Open game.html (or index.html). Click PLAY (or CONTINUE).
  Click a crew portrait, then a station on the map. Drag or arrows pan.
  Speed buttons (or 1/2/3/5/0 keys; Space pauses) run or pause the day.
  Esc opens the menu with Music, SFX and Lighting.

How to play (Linux)
  From this folder:  ./run.sh
  Or open game.html in Firefox / Chrome yourself.

Dev testing
  Open game.html?dev=1 for +1D, +7D, +SEASON, DEC, EVE and 60× warp buttons.
  ?shot=spring|prints|krampus|december|eve|report freezes a key moment.
  ?walk=1 starts Holly walking wrap → bell (for the walk strip).
