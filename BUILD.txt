North Pole — Year 1, tutorial + Esc settings + ElevenLabs SFX (t2113u)
America/Chicago (CST). Rebuilt Saturday Nov 21, 2026, about 5:00 PM CST.

What changed
  Tutorial (t2110u / t2113u): six skippable first-day steps, drawn in the
  official gold UI and driven by Tutorspell (tools/data/game.tutor). It offers
  itself on a new game, never locks the clock, and never blocks the click or
  pan it is teaching. Skip dismisses the whole intro and is remembered.
  Replay Tutorial lives in title OPTIONS and the in-play Esc menu.
  ?tutor=1 forces it; ?tutor=0 turns it off (shots and smokes).

  Esc menu: the in-play pause screen is a gold panel with Music volume, SFX
  volume, Lighting (same storage as title OPTIONS), Replay Tutorial, Resume
  and Menu, plus an ElevenLabs credit line. Esc still deselects first when
  an elf is selected.

  Sound effects: the 13 ElevenLabs picks (tap, assign, jam, fix take 2,
  snack, raid, footprints, quit, eve-sleigh, report, bell, walk loop,
  next-day) are on the Assetspell sfx bus, with compact mp3 + ogg in
  assets/sfx/. Procedural stubs remain as a fallback. The walk loop is
  quiet and will not stack.

  Also: clicking a job while an elf is selected now assigns even if another
  elf is standing on that station (the verb is pick an elf, then a job).

How to play (web)
  Open game.html (or index.html). Click PLAY (or CONTINUE).
  A short tutorial appears on a new game; Skip is always visible.
  Click a crew portrait or an elf in the yard, then a station on the map.
  Drag or arrows pan. Speed buttons (or 1/2/3/5/0 keys; Space pauses).
  Esc deselects, or opens the pause menu when no elf is selected.
