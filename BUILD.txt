What changed since the feel-gate zip
  The workshop is an empty hall. Elves, Santa and Mrs. Claus are real sprites
  that walk, work, rest and face. Krampus stalks in from the doorway (taller
  than Santa) with footprints, a darkened door, drain motes and a Bell chase.
  The report card has a dark veil and a readable panel. New art: cot, wreath
  ward, loaded sleigh, cocoa pot, a proper mood strip, and a blank-faced
  human-eared Quitter. Quota and grades were tuned by seeded sims (see
  BALANCE.md — TUNED GUESSES). Sounds are still placeholders (MISSING-SFX.md).

How to play (web)
  Open game.html in a browser (or open index.html). Click PLAY.
  Click a crew portrait, then a station. Space (or N) advances one day.
  Drag or arrow keys move the overseer cursor. Esc / pause: Music, SFX, Lighting.

How to play (Linux)
  From this folder:  ./run.sh
  Or open game.html in Firefox / Chrome yourself.

Rebuild from source (in the git repo, not required to play the zip)
  python3 tools/build.py
