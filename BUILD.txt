What changed
  The crew strip is compact (56 px). The camera treats the area between the
  top HUD and that strip as the play view, so the sleigh, wrap bench and
  lower elves stay visible. You can still pan, including to the top of the
  map. Default camera shows the yard.
  Speed buttons, the II pause chip, AGAIN / MENU and the report card use the
  gold mockup style. The report is a compact gold-edged panel.
  SFX stay improved procedural placeholders, with a separate SFX volume in
  Options. The ElevenLabs free library could not be reached with existing
  MCP/API access (no catalog of Explore clips; generating would spend
  credits, which was forbidden). Nothing was generated and nothing was spent.
  See AUDIO-CREDITS.md and art/EL-LIBRARY-SEARCH.md.

How to play (web)
  Open game.html (or index.html). Click PLAY (or CONTINUE).
  Click a crew portrait, then a station on the map. Drag or arrows pan.
  Speed buttons (or 1/2/3/5/0 keys; Space pauses) run or pause the day.
  Esc opens the menu with Music, SFX and Lighting.

How to play (Linux)
  From this folder:  ./run.sh
  Or open game.html in Firefox / Chrome yourself.

Dev testing

  ?shot=spring|prints|krampus|december|eve|report freezes a key moment.
  ?walk=1 starts Holly walking wrap → bell (for the walk strip).
